Description:
Video Speed Controller gives you fine-grained control over HTML5 video and audio in Safari — on YouTube, course sites, podcasts, news players, and local files.

Features:
• Speed from 0.07× to 16× with configurable steps
• Keyboard shortcuts (fully remappable): S/D slower/faster, R reset, Z/X seek, G preferred speed, V show/hide, M/J mark/jump
• On-page speed overlay you can drag and restyle
• Remember last speed (optional) and per-site default speeds
• Speed fightback when a site tries to reset playback rate
• Works with both video and audio elements

After installing: open the app, enable the extension in Safari Settings → Extensions, then set Website Access to All Websites. Reload open tabs.

This app contains a Safari Web Extension. It does not collect personal data. Settings stay on your device (and in Safari/iCloud extension storage if you use sync).

Based on the open-source videospeed project (MIT License) by Ilya Grigorik.
