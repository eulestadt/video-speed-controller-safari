Privacy Policy

Video Speed Controller (Safari / macOS)
Last updated: October 10, 2026

Summary

We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal information. The extension runs locally in Safari to adjust HTML5 video and audio playback speed on sites you allow.

Information we do not collect

  • Name, email, account, or payment information
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Browsing history uploaded to our servers (we operate no such servers)
  • Website content uploaded to our servers
  • Health, financial, authentication, or communication content
  • Advertising identifiers or analytics telemetry

Data stored on your device

Preferences such as playback speed, keyboard shortcuts, and per-site rules are saved with the browser extension storage APIs (storage / sync). That data stays in Safari’s extension storage on your devices (and Apple’s sync if you use it). It is not sent to the developer.

Website access

Safari asks you to grant website access. The extension only runs on pages you allow. On those pages it reads the page’s media elements in order to set playbackRate and show an on-page control. That processing is on-device only.

Third parties

No third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs are included.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy by posting a new version at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the project’s GitHub repository or contact the developer via the email associated with the App Store listing.
